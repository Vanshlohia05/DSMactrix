Frequently Asked Questions

Technical Support & Procurement Logistics

What is the difference between OPC 53 and PPC cement?

add_circle

OPC 53 (Ordinary Portland Cement) provides high early strength for structural members. PPC (Portland Pozzolana Cement) contains fly ash, offering better durability, lower heat of hydration, and resistance to chemical attacks, ideal for mass concreting.

Do you provide test certificates for every batch?

add_circle

Yes. Every dispatch is accompanied by a Manufacturer's Test Certificate (MTC) and NABL-accredited lab reports verifying grade, fineness, and setting time parameters.

What is the minimum order quantity for direct factory delivery?

add_circle

Direct factory streams require a minimum order of 15-20 tonnes (full truckload) to optimize logistics costs and ensure batch consistency.
