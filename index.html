PPC (Portland Pozzolana Cement) produces lower heat of hydration, resists dampness better due to pore refinement, protects steel rebar from chemical corrosion, and provides continuous long-term strength gain beyond 28 days.
Fly Ash is an ultra-fine, reactive pozzolanic material. When blended into cement (30% under IS:1489 Part-I), it reacts with free lime to form extra strength-bearing C-S-H gel, improving concrete density, workability, and eco-friendliness.
