DS Power Dhalai Power — Roof & Slab Concreting Specialist

Product Overview: DS Power Dhalai Power is a specialized Portland Pozzolana Cement engineered specifically for roof slabs, load-bearing beams, and residential foundations. Its 30% fly ash matrix lowers the heat of hydration to minimize thermal shrinkage cracks and improve long-term concrete density.

Why Roof Dhalai Requires Specialized PPC

Roof slabs are exposed directly to high tropical temperatures and heavy monsoons in Assam. DS Power Dhalai Power releases hydration heat slowly and evenly, helping prevent thermal contraction micro-fissures and creating a watertight concrete cover that protects internal rebar.

Compaction & Finishing Performance

The pozzolanic micro-particles improve mortar cohesiveness and fluidity under mechanical needle vibration, leading to easy compaction without segregation or honeycombing.
