Why PPC Delivers Superior Structural Slabs

Large continuous concrete pours for roof slabs and dhalai benefit greatly from Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC). Its controlled rate of heat release prevents rapid moisture loss and thermal stress, resulting in higher crack resistance, smoother finishes, and long-lasting monolithic strength.

Foundation Durability in River Basin Subsoils

Foundations and footing concrete in Northeast river valleys face high subsoil dampness and seasonal water table fluctuations. The pozzolanic micro-structure created by DS Power cement locks internal pores, significantly lowering permeability and guarding internal reinforcement against dampness.
