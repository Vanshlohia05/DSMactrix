Why PPC Is Superior for Residential & Commercial Slabs

Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) generates rapid, high heat during hydration, which causes rapid surface evaporation and microscopic shrinkage cracks in wide roof slabs. PPC releases heat slowly and evenly, ensuring sound, crack-free structural slabs.

Foundation Protection Against Assam's Soil Chemistry

The soils in the Brahmaputra river basin often contain moisture and soluble sulfates that attack tricalcium aluminate in OPC. The secondary pozzolanic reaction of DSPOWER cement consumes free calcium hydroxide, making foundation concrete impervious to chemical attack.
