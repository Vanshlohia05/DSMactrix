DSPOWER Premium Cement — High-Strength Structural PPC

Product Overview: DSPOWER Premium Cement is a high-performance Portland Pozzolana Cement formulated with 30% fly ash under IS:1489 Part-1 standards. Supplied directly by K S Enterprise for heavy RCC slabs, load-bearing pillars, and structural engineering across Guwahati and North East India.

Key Parameters & Features

Grade Standard IS:1489 (Part 1) PPC
Fly Ash Blend 30% Pozzolanic Matrix
Strength Growth Continuous Hydraulic Gain
Hydration Heat Low Heat (Crack-Resistant)
Permeability Dense Pore-Refined Structure
Packaging 50 kg Laminated Bag

Engineered for Structural Durability

DSPOWER Premium Cement utilizes reactive pozzolanic materials that combine with free lime during hydration, forming dense Calcium Silicate Hydrate (C-S-H) gel. This lowers concrete permeability, resists subsoil moisture ingress, and protects structural steel reinforcement against corrosion.

Ideal Construction Applications

Recommended for multi-storey residential frames, RCC roof slab casting, foundation pillars, retaining structures, and mass concrete works across Northeast India's high-humidity and monsoon environment.
